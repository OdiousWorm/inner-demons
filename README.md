# inner-demons
Welcome to the README for the Sekiro: Inner Demons gameplay overhaul mod. This document contains comprehensive changelogs for every official release version on Nexusmods, as well as guides for how to access new content, where applicable.

It should go without saying, but to play this mod, please download the file-injection tool "Mod Engine" by Katalash. Download can be found here -> https://www.nexusmods.com/sekiro/mods/6?tab=files

Simply copy and paste the included "mod" folder into your Sekiro directory.

If you have any feedback, please message me on discord @father_gaslight

CHANGELOGS
v1.1.8 released 1-20-2026

New stuff:

- Lone Shadow Masamori the Pureblood
    - Located at the crux of the farmland in Mibu Village
    - Sword and foot based moveset, no gimmicks, hence his name
    - Has a unique look compared to other Lone Shadows
    - Arena becomes enclosed by fog walls upon aggro
    - Drops the "Fresh Pureblood" key item upon death
    - Mandatory for progression to O'rin
- O'rin of the Water rework
    - Remixed moveset
    - O'rin is now mandatory for progression to The Phantom Priestess
    - Arena has been set-dressed with new decorations and other props to give it a fresh look
    - New fog wall blocks your path if you ignore her, no skipping
- New key item: "Fresh Pureblood"
    - Dropped by Masamori
    - Needed to make an offering to the magical stone door which blocks your way just past the Water Mill Sculptor's Idol
    - Includes lore on Masamori
    - Icon is a placeholder. Contact me if you know how to photoshop, lol
- New key item: "Sacrifical Offering"
    - Obtained by making your way into the Mibu Village priest's attic
    - Needed in order to summon The Phantom Priestess
- New Sculptor's Idol: "Wedding Cave Threshold"
    - Placed at the wooden gate threshold to The Phantom Priestess' arena, no annoying runback anymore
- New Sculptor's Idol: "Cursed Farm"
    - Placed at the lowest level of the multi-tier farm located just behind the big ahh tree
- Moved Sculptor's Idol: "Wedding Cave"
    - Now located within the Wedding Cave itself
- New NPC: "Creep"
    - Appears at key moments in the mod to deliver cryptic hints on what to expect / how to beat the ensuing challenge
    - Is very creepy and off-putting about the whole thing

Changed stuff:

- Mibu Village progression has been reworked, as mentioned briefly above
    - Both minibosses, Masamori and O'rin, are now mandatory to progress
    - A key item is now required to summon the level boss which can be found in the latter half of the level
- Tutorial Lone Shadow has been replaced with a Nightjar Ninja, and has been moved to a different location
    - To be able to call Kuro, you need only kill the major threats now, rather than EVERYTHING
- Some button prompts occuring during custom progression moments have been changed to make it more clear what is needed to progress

Bug fixes:

- DAWG / Gyoubu cheese has been fully patched, both methods are now impossible
- Fixed a bug where Shigekichi of the Red Guard's AI was broken and he did not attack
- Fixed a bug where progression could become softlocked in the tutorial if you did not quit and reload
- Fixed a bug where the Sakura Dragon did not take posture damage in the main story progression, thus making the fight take 10 years to complete

Guides:

To get to Lone Shadow Masamori the Pureblood:
- From the "Mibu Village" idol, make your way to the farmland behind the great tree surrounded by several ground grabby guys, he can be found at the shrine in the back

Thank you for your support. As always, feel free to sub to me at https://patreon.com/FatherGaslight

GUIDES

To get to Seven Ashina Spears - Father Gaslight's Cousin:
- Reach the "Ashina Castle Gate Fortress" idol in the pre-invasion phase of the game. He can be found in the middle of the court surrounded by a group of enemies.

To get to Dragonspring Brute:
- Go to the Hirata Estate. Upon spawning in, he can be found at the secluded end of the main bridge to the estate, patrolling the blocked off entrance with a big stick.

To get to Shady Bamboo Monkey:
- Reach the "Estate Path" idol. Climb over the wall into the area to the left, he can be found at the end just past the pissing bandit, sitting amongst a large pile of bodies.

To get to Ministry Elite - Greg of the Twin Fangs:
- Reach the 1st invasion phase of the game. He can be just outside of the "Abandoned Dungeon Entrance" idol, on the bridge leading to the castle moat. He is hard to miss.

To get to Ministry Elite - Akiyama of the Twin Fangs:
- Reach the 2nd invasion phase of the game. He can be found at the end of the progression to make it to the Demon Ash Withered Gaslight, guarding the idol which teleports you to the Flames of Hatred arena/idol.
